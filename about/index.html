What ScriptNovaa builds

ScriptNovaa develops and operates the Nebula workspace: its product names, chat experience, assistant configurations, routing, account controls, and connected tools. Nebula Daily, Ultra, Spark, and Duo are ScriptNovaa-operated assistant products. Duo is a ScriptNovaa-designed collaboration workflow that prepares and reviews an answer.

Some responses use foundation models hosted by external AI services. That service relationship does not make the provider the owner of Nebula. It also does not mean every underlying model was trained from scratch by ScriptNovaa. See Nebula models for the distinction between the product layer and the underlying model.

What you can do with Nebula

Chat with a model for the task

Daily is tuned for concise everyday replies; Ultra handles general work; Spark is a quick assistant; Duo combines two assistant responses for a joint review. D2 is a restricted preview.

Use a workspace, not only a prompt box

Depending on account and availability, Nebula includes Cloud tools, a browser extension for PC mode, image creation, saved work, developer APIs, voice, and account-level usage controls.

Keep control of context

Saved chats, optional cross-chat memory, training sharing, and external review have separate controls. Read the privacy policy before connecting accounts or sharing sensitive data.

Built for clear expectations

Nebula is experimental software. Responses can be wrong, availability and limits can change, and tool actions should be checked in their actual destination. The Trust & transparency page describes the product identity, model relationships, and what is and is not independently certified.
