Nebula by ScriptNovaa

Your AI workspace

Nebula is ScriptNovaa’s workspace for writing, coding, explanations and planning. Choose Ultra for versatile assistance, Spark for quick everyday help, or Duo for independent drafts and a shared review. Experimental models may also appear in the model menu.

Context you control

Ultra receives the current conversation. With cross-chat memory enabled, it can also receive your saved facts and bounded excerpts from up to three recent, unarchived conversations. Private file-context chats are excluded from those excerpts. Turn memory off in Settings → Memory to stop including this context.

Tools and task progress

Signed-in users can use the tools enabled for their account, such as webpage reading, calculation, weather and cloud tasks. Independent read-only lookups can run together. Task drafts require confirmation in Cloud before scheduling. “How this answer was prepared” shows context counts and tool activity; complex answers can include a short approach summary.

Choose how you work

Save conversations, control memory, export your chats and use account tools in Nebula Cloud. Duo checks complex requests with both Spark and Ultra, then prepares one answer. Simple greetings use a direct response. If one assistant is busy, Duo can return an available answer with an explicit notice that joint review is incomplete. Collaboration summaries describe completed steps, not private reasoning. A shared review does not guarantee correctness.

Account levels

Everyone starts at Normal. Medium unlocks after 20 successful replies and one day; High after 150 replies and seven days. Good standing is required. Medium and High include Spark and Duo. Normal and Medium have a 10-hour credit window; High has a 1-hour window. Every level also has a weekly allowance. View your progress and exact reset times in Settings → Usage. An owner may record restrictions or reviewed overrides; AI opinions never award developer access.
