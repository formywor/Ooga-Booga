Official product identity

Nebula is developed and operated by ScriptNovaa. The official web app is scriptnovaa.com. Nebula by ScriptNovaa is an AI chat and productivity workspace; it is not a gaming cheat, script executor, or download launcher.

ScriptNovaa owns and operates the Nebula product, assistant configurations, product names, web experience, and connected workflows. The product is not affiliated with similarly named services unless a relationship is explicitly announced here.

ScriptNovaa does not claim endorsement or certification by Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, or a security auditor. Third-party “verified” labels are controlled by the issuing platform, not by Nebula’s website.

How the AI layer works

ScriptNovaa products

Nebula Daily, Ultra, Spark, and Duo are ScriptNovaa-operated assistant products. ScriptNovaa builds their routing, instructions, user experience, and tool integration.

Underlying models

Some responses use foundation models hosted by external AI services. Those model weights and services remain separate from ScriptNovaa’s Nebula products. Nebula does not claim that every underlying model was trained from scratch by ScriptNovaa. See the model guide.

Preview features

D2 is a limited experimental preview. It can be unavailable or change during research. PC, Cloud, and image features have their own account, device, and usage requirements.

Data and security commitments

Clear data controls

Read what is sent to inference services, how optional training sharing and external review work, and how to export or delete data in the privacy policy. New accounts start with optional sharing and review off; each setting can be changed separately.

Connected account access

Google and browser connections request the permissions needed for the feature you choose. Review the consent screen and disconnect access from the relevant account or Nebula settings when you no longer need it.

Independent audits

No independent security certification or full third-party audit is claimed on this page. If an external audit is commissioned and its findings can be published, this page will link to the report and its scope.
