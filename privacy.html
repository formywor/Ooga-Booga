Data & privacy

What Nebula receives

When you ask a question, chat context is processed by the AI services supporting your selected Nebula model. Duo shares this context with both of its assistants to prepare and review an answer. This processing is needed to answer and is separate from optional training sharing and answer review. Files you select may contribute extracted text to that request.

Conversations and memory

Guest conversations are kept for the current visit. Signed-in conversations and account settings are stored for your Nebula account. When cross-chat memory is on, saved facts and short excerpts from up to three recent, unarchived chats may be sent as additional context. Private file-context conversations are excluded from cross-chat excerpts. You can turn memory off, delete saved facts, export chats or delete chats in Settings. Turning memory off stops its use; it does not delete saved facts.

Training sharing and answer reviews

Training sharing and external answer reviews are enabled for new accounts under the current defaults. You can turn each off in Settings → Data. Turning sharing off stops future contributions. Delete shared training data removes stored contributions and turns sharing off; it cannot reverse training already completed. Optional reviews may send eligible conversation content to the configured review service. Private-context turns are excluded from sharing and external review.

Connected services

Account connections and enabled search, weather, voice or email tools may send the inputs needed for that action to those services. Cloud shows connected services and task status. Ultra does not receive browser passwords or arbitrary access to your device.

Deletion and retention

Saved account data remains until removed through the available controls; Nebula does not currently promise a fixed automatic deletion period. Provider processing and retention also follow each provider’s terms. These controls do not promise deletion from provider systems or previously trained weights.

Images and Cloud workspace

Only photos you choose to send are processed by Nebula’s image analysis service. Camera preview stays on your device until you capture and send a photo; it is not a live feed to the AI. Image files are not kept in conversation history, training contributions or external answer reviews. Your typed or transcribed question and the answer may be saved to your signed-in conversation.

Keeping photos is optional. Save to account or the account-gallery option stores resized images privately with your account for your other devices. The account gallery supports up to 200 images and 100 MB; delete images there when you no longer want them. This is separate from device storage. Cloud code and workspace files are processed in an isolated environment without your passwords or service credentials. Saved workspace files and recent results remain associated with your account. Each code run or public-page inspection uses additional credits. The device gallery stores resized photos in this browser under your account and does not sync them to other devices. You can remove a saved photo or use Save copy to download it. Temporary photos are cleared when you close the call or change accounts.
