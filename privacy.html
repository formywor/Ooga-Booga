Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

What ScriptNovaa stores

ScriptNovaa stores the account username, protected PIN and recovery-code credentials, point and token history, referral history, reward attempts, support tickets, registered-device identifiers in hashed form, fraud and risk status, and browser-session timing needed to operate Share Browser.

Device and network information

Share Browser uses a one-device registration system. The launcher creates a device proof from Windows identifiers and sends it over HTTPS. The API stores a keyed hash rather than the original identifiers. Truncated IP information and request timing may be used for rate limiting, referral checks, reward checks, and abuse prevention.

Advertising and outside pages

Sponsored links and notification advertising may be supplied by third parties. Opening them can allow those providers to process information under their own policies. ScriptNovaa does not control the content or privacy practices of outside pages.

Browser sessions

The API stores token ownership, selected browser mode, session start and end times, heartbeat status, and the reason a session ended. Temporary browser profiles are created locally and removed by the launcher when a managed session ends.

Sign-in storage

The website stores a private sign-in token in the browser’s local and session storage so the user remains signed in until signing out. The launcher stores its paired-account token and device proof in the current Windows user’s registry. Account recovery revokes existing sign-in tokens.

Security and retention

Direct public access to the Realtime Database is disabled. Trusted API routes perform sensitive changes. Operational records may be retained for security, fraud review, account support, and enforcing product rules.

Your choices

You can stop using Share Browser, sign out of the website, avoid optional sponsored links, and contact Support about account or privacy questions. Losing both the PIN and recovery code may make account recovery impossible.
