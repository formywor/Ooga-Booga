What we need to host your website

  • Ownership or written permission to host all code, images, music and branding.
  • A description, intended audience, contact method and repository or preview link. Never include secrets.
  • Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript source with a build guide and list of external services. Servers, databases, arbitrary proxies and uploads need a separate review.
  • Privacy, terms, accessibility and safe-content policies; disclose ads and any tracking. No deceptive downloads or permission prompts.
  • For your own domain: DNS access to add our unique TXT verification record and CNAME routing record, then HTTPS provisioning.

The first hosting release serves a fixed ScriptNovaa demonstration, not uploaded third-party code. We do not yet have outside sponsored partners. Approved proposals can be prepared for later onboarding.

New sponsor website sessions have no countdown. Older managed-hosting links retain their original Galaxy expiry. Content already received cannot be remotely erased. Replit and Vercel are deployment providers, not announced sponsors.
