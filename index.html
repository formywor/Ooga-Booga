Data & privacy

Nebula Ultra uses an external AI service to generate replies. Your messages and relevant chat context are sent to that service when you chat, including when training sharing and optional reviews are off. Those settings control additional data use, not the processing needed to answer you.

Allow new messages and replies to be saved for review. Anonymous topic counts guide verified practice in scheduled training. Replies are not automatically treated as correct answers. On by default for new accounts; you can turn it off here.

Allow eligible conversation messages and Nebula's replies to be sent to a third-party AI service for review. Reviews help identify mistakes. On by default for new accounts; you can turn this off independently of training sharing.

Checking review availability.

With training sharing on, corrections that pass additional AI and text checks can be used in scheduled training. Reviews do not instantly update Nebula or guarantee mistake-free answers. Reviews are batched after a few replies, later during rapid conversations, or after a chat becomes idle. Idle checks run while Nebula is open and resume when you return. Earlier model versions and messages sent with reviews off are excluded. Turning this off stops future review requests. With training sharing off, reviews are not saved as training examples.

Avoid sharing sensitive information. Turning sharing off stops future contributions and excludes your stored contributions from new topic summaries. Deleting shared data cannot undo completed training. Cross-chat memory is a separate choice.

Connected tools and scheduled tasks

Search queries and weather locations are sent to the corresponding lookup services. Scheduled email details and results are saved to your account separately from chat training sharing. Google connections grant only the permissions shown by Google, and can be disconnected in Cloud workspace. No Google password is collected.
