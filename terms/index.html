Using Nebula

Experimental AI

Nebula provides AI-generated assistance and experimental research features. Answers can be incorrect or incomplete. Check generated code and important factual claims before using them.

Your content and permissions

Only submit content and connect accounts you are authorized to use. Keep passwords, API keys and other credentials out of chats. You control optional memory, training sharing and external answer reviews in Settings.

Responsible use

Do not use Nebula for exploitation, violence, credential theft, unauthorized access, fraud, harassment or spam. Use connected accounts and tools only with permission. Safety protections apply to every Nebula assistant. AI assistance does not replace qualified professional advice for important decisions.

Tools and availability

Available models, connected tools and usage limits may change. An answer or task proposal does not establish that an external action occurred; check its actual status in the workspace. Cloud tasks require your confirmation before scheduling.

Data controls

Read the Data & privacy page for what is sent to inference and review services and how to export or delete stored conversations, memories and contributions.
