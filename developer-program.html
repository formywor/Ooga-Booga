Revenue sharing

Grow with what you build.

Shares apply to eligible profit or advertising revenue actually received and verified by ScriptNovaa, after refunds, fees, invalid traffic, taxes, and other agreed deductions.

Browser Creator4%-48%

Potential share of eligible profit generated by your browser. The level depends on performance, verified profit, compliance, activity, and time in the program.

Long-term Creatorup to 79%

Creators active and in good standing for approximately one to two years or longer may become eligible for a higher negotiated share.

Domain Partner25%-89%

Potential share of eligible advertising profit from an approved ScriptNova experience operating on your domain.

These ranges describe the planned beta model, not promised income. Final shares require a written agreement and may vary by traffic quality, advertising-provider rules, region, expenses, account standing, and program changes.
