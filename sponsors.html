Become a sponsor

Submit a Sponsorship / website partner application in the Developer Portal. Include your contact details, preview URL, intended audience, and written permission to display your website and branding. Never send passwords or private API keys.

To host your pages

Supply the complete static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images and fonts, a build guide, all page routes, and a list of external services. Confirm copyright, privacy, tracking and advertising permissions. Forms, logins, databases and server code require a separate technical review. DNS does not upload your files. Uploaded partner code is not automatically deployed in this release.

To embed your existing website

Provide an HTTPS URL and authorize framing from https://scriptnovaa.com in your Content-Security-Policy frame-ancestors settings. Conflicting X-Frame-Options headers can prevent framing. Every destination page must permit embedding; we cannot bypass these protections. Test links, mobile layout, cookies, forms and sign-in. Embedded scripts are sandboxed and cannot access ScriptNovaa account data.

Approval and availability

Staff review safety, ownership, navigation and compatibility before listing a sponsor. New approved listings appear from our API without another Galaxy download. The ScriptNovaa listing is our own demonstration; it is not a claim of an outside sponsorship. Sessions do not award points or generate automatic ad clicks.
