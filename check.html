      What this tool can—and cannot—tell you.

      01 / PATTERNS

      AI-like writing signals

      Sentence uniformity, repeated phrases, vocabulary repetition, formulaic transitions, and results from available online detectors are compared.

      02 / HIDDEN DATA

      Invisible character scan

      Zero-width characters, bidirectional controls, Unicode tags, private-use characters, soft hyphens, and other unusual controls are reported with positions.

      03 / LIMITS

      No false certainty

      Human writing can look automated and edited AI writing can look human. Short samples are especially unreliable. Never punish someone using this score alone.

      This page examines pasted text. It cannot inspect hidden layers, revision history, document metadata, images, or watermarks in an original Word or PDF file.
