What is distinctive about Nebula?

A workspace layer

Nebula combines its chat products with account settings, usage visibility, an API, Cloud workspace, and a browser extension for PC mode. Not every capability is enabled for every user.

Different assistant experiences

Daily favors short, lower-context replies; Ultra is the general assistant; Spark offers quick help; Duo runs a multi-assistant review workflow. These are ScriptNovaa product experiences, not a claim that every base model is owned or trained by ScriptNovaa.

Open about trade-offs

Nebula does not claim to match the training scale, integrations, or reliability programs of frontier AI providers. Compare the tools on your own tasks, and check each product’s data practices before sharing sensitive information.

Which one should you choose?

Pick the assistant whose tools, privacy terms, access model, and answers fit your work. For high-stakes decisions, verify claims with primary sources regardless of the model. Nebula’s model guide, trust page, and privacy policy explain its current setup.

No public benchmark in this page proves that one product is “the smartest.” Model versions, routes, account limits, and features change over time.
