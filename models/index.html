Meet the assistants

Nebula Daily

Fast, compact help for everyday questions. Daily keeps a shorter recent context and is the default for new accounts and guest sessions. Configured backup inference services can take over during provider outages or capacity limits.

Nebula Ultra

A general-purpose assistant for writing, coding, explanations, and longer tasks. It can use the workspace tools available to your account.

Nebula Spark

A quick assistant for concise answers, brainstorming, and everyday help. Availability depends on your account level and current service configuration.

Nebula Duo

A ScriptNovaa-designed collaboration workflow that asks two assistants to prepare and review an answer, then presents a concise result. If a reviewer is unavailable, the response says when a joint review did not finish.

Nebula D2 Preview

An experimental research assistant with restricted preview access. D2 is still under development and may be unavailable. It is not represented as a finished general-release product.

Nebula Cloud and PC

Optional tools let eligible users work with webpages, code, and connected browser tasks. Access depends on the selected mode, account, device connection, and confirmation settings.

Who owns and operates Nebula?

Nebula is a ScriptNovaa product. ScriptNovaa develops and operates the Nebula workspace, assistant experiences, product names, routing, interfaces, and connected tools. “Nebula Daily,” “Nebula Ultra,” “Nebula Spark,” and “Nebula Duo” describe ScriptNovaa products and configurations.

Some replies are generated using foundation models hosted by external AI services. Those underlying model weights and provider services are separate from ScriptNovaa’s Nebula products; not every Nebula assistant is a foundation model trained from scratch by ScriptNovaa. The service’s routing can change, and the exact provider is not promised as a permanent product feature. See Data & privacy for how conversation content is processed.

Questions about Nebula models

Are all Nebula models trained from scratch?

No. Nebula is a product family. Some Nebula assistants use ScriptNovaa’s orchestration and behavior on top of externally hosted foundation models. We do not claim that every underlying model’s weights were trained by ScriptNovaa.

Does ScriptNovaa own Nebula Duo and Daily?

ScriptNovaa owns and operates the Duo workflow and Daily assistant product. That product ownership is distinct from ownership of the underlying foundation model weights or external inference services.

Is Nebula affiliated with other products named Nebula?

Nebula by ScriptNovaa is identified by the official domain scriptnovaa.com. It is not presented as an affiliate of similarly named products.
