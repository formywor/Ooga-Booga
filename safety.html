ScriptNovaa Safety

Speak freely. Treat people safely.

Chat includes reporting, blocking, muting, link filtering, spam limits, and recorded administrator moderation.

01

Control your space

Report a message, block an account, mute someone without blocking them, or disable private messages in Settings.

02

Warnings are clear

A filtered unsafe or repeated-spam message creates a chat warning. Two active warnings within 30 days pause chat access for two days. Your browser and account remain usable.

03

Private means limited

Private messages are visible to participants and may be reviewed by authorized administrators for safety. Never send PINs, recovery codes, or browser tokens.

04

Retention is visible

Public messages automatically expire after 48 hours. Administrative safety records may last longer when needed to document a report or moderation decision.
