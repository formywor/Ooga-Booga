Voice cancellation

Say “cancel”, wait for the admin-code prompt, then say “two two four four”. The alarm remains armed until the code is accepted. Supported desktop Chrome/Edge required. The browser speech service may process microphone audio online.

Saved clips

Event clips include the previous 10 seconds (when buffered), microphone audio when enabled, and a 5-second tail after the last detection. Long events are saved in separate, playable parts of up to 25 seconds. A disconnect tail is marked offline; unavailable footage cannot be recovered. The API keeps the latest 20 clips, each up to 1 MB, for up to 7 days. Download clips you want to keep.

Activity

Keep this PC awake and this dashboard visible. The armed setting survives refreshes and reconnects. After refreshing, press Enable PC audio if the browser blocks sound or buffering. Closing the dashboard stops monitoring; background tabs may be throttled. Camera capture may stop when an iPhone locks or Safari is backgrounded. Disconnects cancel alarms. Detection must be tested in your actual lighting and camera positions.
