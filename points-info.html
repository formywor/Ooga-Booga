Points and rewards

How claiming points works

Points can be earned through sponsored links and qualified referrals. Available points can be exchanged for timed Share Browser tokens.

01

Open the link

Press the sponsored-link button yourself. Ads never open automatically.

02

Wait for review

The claim wait ranges from 0–14 minutes. Established clean accounts usually wait less; new or previously flagged accounts wait longer.

03

Return and claim

Return to Tokens and press Claim. A valid completed attempt adds 0.5 point.

Reward limits

Reward per sponsored link0.5 point

Repeating the same campaignAllowed with a new valid attempt

Referral rewards

A referred user receives 2 pending points. They become available after registering their computer and passing basic device and IP checks.

A qualified referral awards 3 points for signup, 4 additional points for the first valid session, and a 1-point review bonus: 8 points total.

How wait times are selected

Established accounts with valid session history and no fraud flags receive a 0–4 minute wait. New clean accounts receive 5–14 minutes. Accounts previously flagged or connected to reversed fake-referral rewards receive 12–14 minutes.

Fair-use rules

Automated visits, replaying claims, fake referrals, multiple accounts, or bypassing limits may reverse rewards and apply a 2-point fraud penalty. An incomplete referral is not fraud and receives no penalty.
