Points and rewards

How claiming points works

Points are ScriptNovaa product credits. Earn them through browser setup, sponsored links, and qualified referrals, then exchange available points for timed Share Browser tokens.

01

Open the link

Press the sponsored-link button yourself. Reward pages never open automatically.

02

Wait for review

The claim wait ranges from 0–14 minutes. Established clean accounts usually wait less; new or previously flagged accounts wait longer.

03

Return and claim

Return to Tokens and press Claim. A valid completed attempt adds 0.5 point.

SYSTEM REWARD

First computer setup

Connecting the first approved computer awards 2 points once. The server records the reward, so refreshing or reconnecting cannot award it twice. Referred users’ original 2-point setup reward is counted as this same bonus.

Reward limits

Reward per sponsored link0.5 point

Referral rewards

The referred user’s 2-point setup reward becomes available after their computer passes the basic device and IP checks.

A qualified referral awards the referrer 3 points after device approval, 4 additional points for the first valid session, and a 1-point review bonus: 8 points total.

How wait times are selected

Established accounts with valid session history and no fraud flags receive a 0–4 minute wait. New clean accounts receive 5–14 minutes. Accounts previously flagged or connected to reversed fake-referral rewards receive 12–14 minutes.

Fair-use rules

Automated visits, replaying claims, fake referrals, multiple accounts, proxies used to bypass limits, or other limit evasion may reverse rewards and apply a 2-point fraud penalty. An incomplete referral is not fraud and receives no penalty.
