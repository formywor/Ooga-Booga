Standard

Multi-use

Reopen the same active session on the same connected computer until its purchased time expires.

Shared account

Galaxy automatically checks the computer connection already saved by Share Browser or Project Z. It uses separate GALAXY- tokens.

Search privacy and partner disclosure

Snova relays the words in your search query to a search provider. It is not a full website proxy, VPN, or IP-hiding service. When you open a result, your browser connects directly to that website using your normal public IP.

The current ScriptNovaa partner capsule is a demonstration owned by ScriptNovaa. It does not award points, create simulated advertising interactions, or represent an outside paid partner.

Galaxy uses dedicated browser profiles but cannot override website rules, school or workplace policies, Windows security controls, or the privacy practices of destination websites.
